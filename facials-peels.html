Skin Rejuvenation

Medi-Facials & Skin Rejuvenation

Unlike traditional salon facials that offer temporary surface-level hydration, our Medi-Facials are scientifically formulated, medically supervised treatments that target specific skin concerns using advanced technology and potent, medical-grade active ingredients.

01

Hydrafacial — The Ultimate Deep Cleanse & Glow

The gold standard in non-invasive skin resurfacing, using a patented 3-step vortex-fusion technology to cleanse, exfoliate and hydrate simultaneously. It gently vacuums out impurities, debris and blackheads from the pores while infusing the skin with nutrient-rich serums tailored to your needs — hyaluronic acid, antioxidants and peptides.

Best for: Instant radiance, deep hydration and overall skin health with zero downtime.
02

Microdermabrasion — Crystal & Diamond-Tip Resurfacing

A highly effective mechanical exfoliation that polishes away dull outer layers to reveal a smoother, fresher complexion. Diamond-tip microdermabrasion uses a precise abrasive wand to sweep away dead cells, ideal for sensitive areas like around the eyes; crystal microdermabrasion uses a fine spray of medical-grade micro-crystals to lift away uneven texture.

Best for: Improving uneven skin tone, refining mild acne scarring, minimising fine lines and smoothing rough texture.
03

Advanced Acne Cleanup Facials

Designed for acne-prone, congested and oily skin, this targeted clinical treatment calms active breakouts and purifies the skin matrix. It combines professional, aseptic extractions of stubborn comedones — blackheads and whiteheads — with deep-cleansing enzymes and anti-inflammatory, antibacterial serums.

Best for: Clearing clogged pores, regulating excess sebum, reducing active acne inflammation and preventing future breakouts.
04

Custom Clinical Chemical Peels

A versatile, dermatologist-formulated treatment that accelerates skin-cell turnover using safe acidic solutions. Depending on your concern, we select from medical-grade peels — Salicylic, Glycolic, Lactic or specialised combination peels — that dissolve the bonds holding damaged surface cells together, allowing a flawless new skin layer to emerge.

Best for: Correcting stubborn hyperpigmentation, treating active acne, fading acne marks (PIH), reducing sun damage and restoring a uniform skin tone.

Light-Based Therapy

Advanced Laser Facials & Carbon Peels

For deep structural rejuvenation, uneven pigmentation and immediate luminosity, our medical laser facials use precise wavelengths of light to stimulate cellular repair, shatter pigment clusters and boost collagen from the inside out.

01

The Hollywood Carbon Peel (Q-Switched Laser)

Also known as the Hollywood Facial or Charcoal Peel, this is a pain-free treatment delivering instant radiance with zero downtime — the ultimate pre-event facial for a flawless, porcelain finish. A layer of medical-grade liquid carbon is applied to the face and penetrates deep into the pores, bonding with dead skin cells, excess sebum and impurities. A high-intensity Q-Switched Nd:YAG laser is then passed over the skin; the light is attracted to the black carbon particles and vaporises them instantly, blasting away bound impurities, exfoliating the surface and deeply clearing the pores.

Best for: Instant glow, refined pores and reduced sebaceous-gland activity.
02

Laser Toning & Rejuvenation Facials

The clinical gold standard for stubborn pigmentation, melasma, dark spots and uneven tone. It uses low-fluence — gentle but highly effective — laser energy in ultra-short pulses that create an acoustic wave to safely shatter deep-seated melanin into microscopic particles your body naturally flushes away, while the deep thermal warmth stimulates fibroblasts to kickstart fresh collagen.

Best for: Correcting stubborn hyperpigmentation, fading dark spots, sun damage and post-acne marks (PIH) while restoring a bright, uniform complexion.
