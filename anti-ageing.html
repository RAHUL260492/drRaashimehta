Anti-Ageing & Lifting

Anti-Ageing & Structural Rejuvenation

As skin ages, its structural foundations shift — collagen and elastin slow, and deeper fat pads, muscles and bone lose volume. We restore firmness and lift with natural-looking, layered treatments.

Lift, Tighten & Rejuvenate

Anti-Ageing, Lifting & Structural Rejuvenation

01

Laser Toning & Radiofrequency (RF) Tightening

A non-invasive combination that lifts contours with zero social downtime. RF Tightening delivers deep, controlled thermal energy into the subdermal layers, instantly contracting lax collagen fibres while stimulating fibroblasts to build fresh collagen over the following weeks. Laser Toning uses ultra-fast pulses to target deep-seated dullness, improving clarity and elasticity.

Best for: Lifting sagging jowls, defining a soft jawline, tightening mild laxity and restoring a firm, youthful bounce.
02

Advanced Microcurrent Facial (Myolift™ Technology)

Often called a non-surgical facelift, this targets the true foundation of sagging — the underlying muscles. Premium Myolift™ technology delivers low-grade electrical microcurrents that mimic the body's own ionic signals, using a dual-probe system to re-educate elongated, sagging muscles to lift while relaxing hyper-contracted muscles that cause expression wrinkles. This biological stimulation boosts ATP (cellular energy) production significantly.

Best for: Immediate facial contouring, lifting drooping eyebrows, sculpting cheekbones and defining the jawline.
03

Regenerative Microneedling with PRP (The Matrix Reboot)

A natural, biological approach to cellular youth. Medical-grade microneedling creates thousands of microscopic pathways which we flood with autologous Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) isolated from your own blood, rich in growth factors, triggering intense accelerated cellular repair.

Best for: Improving skin density, smoothing fine lines, refining crepey texture under the eyes or on the neck, and a radiant, deep-lit glow.
04

Fractional CO₂ Laser Skin Resurfacing

The gold standard for comprehensive texture overhaul and deep dermal remodeling. Targeted micro-beams vaporise columns of aged, damaged cells while leaving surrounding tissue intact; the deep heat forces immediate contraction of the skin matrix and a surge in long-term collagen.

Best for: Erasing deep-set wrinkles, smoothing perioral lines and crow's feet, reversing severe sun damage and tightening the skin surface.
05

Advanced Clinical Injectables & Biostimulators

For precise facial optimisation where topical skincare cannot reach.

  • Botulinum Toxin (Wrinkle Relaxers) — precisely placed micro-injections that relax hyperactive muscles to smooth forehead lines, frown lines and crow's feet.
  • Dermal Fillers (Structural Volume) — premium cross-linked Hyaluronic Acid expertly placed to lift sagging cheeks, smooth deep nasolabial folds and contour the jawline.
  • Skin Boosters & PDRN (Cellular Hydrators) — micro-droplets of non-crosslinked HA and salmon-DNA fragments across the skin matrix to lock in moisture and repair cells at a molecular level.
Best for: Softening expression lines, restoring lost volume and deep cellular hydration.
