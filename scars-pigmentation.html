Acne Scars & Pigmentation

Complex, multi-layered concerns need a tailored, multi-modality approach — combining treatments that break down rigid tissue with technologies that rebuild collagen for dramatically smoother, re-textured skin.

Advanced Scar Revision

Acne Scar Revision & Tissue Remodeling

Acne scars form when severe inflammation disrupts the skin's natural healing, leaving uneven texture or deep depressions. Because scars exist at varying depths and involve different tissue types, a generic approach cannot deliver optimal results — true scar revision relies on a customised, combination protocol.

01

Fractional CO₂ Laser Resurfacing — the clinical gold standard

The clinical gold standard for structural skin remodeling and deep texture correction. It delivers micro-beams of high-energy light into the skin, creating thousands of microscopic treatment zones while leaving the surrounding tissue untouched — triggering an accelerated natural wound-healing response.

Best for: Smoothing deep boxcar and rolling scars, refining coarse texture and tightening the skin matrix.
02

Medical Microneedling — Collagen Induction Therapy

A minimally invasive treatment that awakens the skin's natural healing potential. Sterile, medical-grade micro-needles create precise micro-channels to break down old, rigid scar tissue and stimulate fibroblasts to produce fresh, organised collagen.

Best for: Improving shallow to medium depressed scars, reducing pore size and enhancing suppleness.
03

Autologous PRP — Platelet-Rich Plasma Therapy

Harnesses your body's own cellular power to accelerate tissue repair and supercharge collagen synthesis. A small sample of your blood is processed to isolate a concentrated pool of growth factors, then applied or micro-injected into scarred areas to speed healing and enhance structural results.

Best for: Accelerating post-laser recovery, healing stubborn scar tissue and improving overall tone and volume.
04

Advanced Subcision — Dermo-Surgical Scar Release

A hands-on surgical technique for deep, bound-down rolling scars. Depressed scars are pulled down by tough fibrous bands anchoring the skin to deeper structures; using an ultra-fine micro-needle inserted horizontally under the skin, we gently release these bands to instantly lift the depression.

Best for: Tethered rolling scars and deep craters that creams or surface treatments cannot reach.
05

Deep Hydration Skin Boosters — an injectable moisturiser

Acts as an internal, injectable moisturiser to plump and smooth indented surfaces. Micro-injections of highly fluid, stabilised Hyaluronic Acid (HA) are delivered into the dermal layers to spread evenly, deeply hydrating the matrix and softening sharp scar edges.

Best for: Softening atrophic (depressed) acne scars, restoring structural hydration and a smoother, plumper finish.

Melanin Control

Advanced Pigmentation Solutions

Hyperpigmentation — melasma, sun spots, freckles or post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH) from old acne — occurs when skin cells produce localised excesses of melanin. We match the treatment to the depth and cause of the pigment.

01

Advanced Laser Toning

Ultra-short pulses of a Q-Switched Nd:YAG laser deliver targeted acoustic energy deep into the skin. Rather than harsh heat, the laser safely shatters melanin clusters into microscopic particles your body gradually flushes away.

Best for: Melasma, deep sun damage, stubborn post-acne marks and an overall brighter tone.
02

Regenerative PRP for Pigmentation

Uses your body's natural growth factors to regulate cell signalling and repair damaged skin architecture. A concentrated pool of platelets and healing proteins from your own blood accelerates tissue renewal and helps regulate tyrosinase activity to naturally fade dark spots.

Best for: Post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation, uneven texture and restoring a healthy, radiant bounce.
03

Cellular Therapy — Microneedling with Exosomes & PDRN

The cutting edge of regenerative dermatology, combining mechanical micro-channels with powerful cellular messengers. Exosomes are microscopic, cell-derived vesicles packed with signalling proteins and growth factors that instruct skin cells to repair and renew; PDRN (Polydeoxyribonucleotide) is premium salmon-derived DNA that accelerates cellular repair and calms chronic inflammation.

Best for: Resistant melasma, compromised skin barriers and advanced global skin rejuvenation.
