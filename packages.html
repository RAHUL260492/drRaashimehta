Signature Curations

Signature Curations & Occasion Packages

Milestones deserve a radiant celebration. Our specialty packages combine advanced energy-based devices, clinical medi-facials and cell-signaling serums under strict medical protocols — for targeted, dramatic transformations timed to life's most memorable moments.

Each curation is a structured, multi-session journey — not a single treatment — sequenced by Dr. Raashi to peak exactly when you need it most. Choose the milestone that matches your moment.

01

The Bride-to-Be Radiance Protocol

A flawless, deeply hydrated, photo-ready complexion for your special day.

A wedding requires meticulous planning, and your skin is no exception. This bridal blueprint is structured around your timeline to clear deep congestion, smooth uneven texture and impart an inner, candle-lit glow that wears beautifully under high-definition makeup.

The Modalities: deep-cleansing Hydrafacials to vacuum out stubborn impurities, gentle Laser Toning to shatter micro-pigmentation and even skin tone, finished with Custom Combination Peels to eliminate surface dullness for an airbrushed look.
The Timeline: for optimal results, begin 3 to 6 months before the wedding to comfortably allow for cell renewal.
Best for: Universal bridal brightening, pore refinement and a flawless, long-lasting wedding glow.
02

The New Mommy Skin Makeover

A restorative, completely safe protocol to reclaim skin bounce, fade hormonal pigmentation and refresh tired eyes.

The postpartum period brings immense joy, but shifting hormones, sleep deprivation and physical stress can show on the skin as melasma (the "mask of pregnancy"), sudden breakouts and structural exhaustion. This deeply restorative makeover focuses on safe, clinically sound cellular replenishment.

The Modalities: cell-toning Myolift™ Microcurrent to drain postpartum puffiness and lift facial contours by boosting cellular ATP, combined with ultra-soothing, medical-grade barrier-repair facials and non-ablative light therapies to safely target hormonal pigmentation without aggressive downtime.
Safety note: every ingredient and technology is strictly cross-verified for safety, making it completely compatible with nursing mothers.
Best for: Fading melasma, tightening soft-tissue volume loss, contouring the jawline and erasing signs of fatigue around the eyes.
03

The Clear Horizon — Poreless Skin Treatment

A high-performance protocol to drastically shrink enlarged pores, smooth texture and control heavy oil.

Enlarged pores are often structural anchors caused by chronic oil congestion or a loss of collagen around the pore walls. This targeted package goes beyond basic extractions to structurally remodel the surface and regulate active sebaceous glands.

The Modalities: the deep thermal action of the Hollywood Carbon Peel (vaporising deep sebum and instantly tightening pore structure) combined with targeted Medical Microneedling or Fractional CO₂ laser in micro-pulses to rebuild structural collagen around the pore walls, forcing them to contract.
The Finish: stabilised Hyaluronic Acid Skin Boosters flood the extracellular matrix with internal hydration, smoothing sharp indentations for a velvety, refined canvas.
Best for: Oily and combination skin, rough texture, open pores and persistent congestion.
