Laser Hair Reduction

Shaving, waxing and plucking are temporary — and leave razor burns, ingrown hairs and constant upkeep. Laser Hair Reduction targets the follicle at the root for safe, long-lasting smoothness.

The Technology

Triple Wavelength Laser

We use advanced Triple Wavelength Laser technology — the gold standard in modern hair reduction.

Three wavelengths, one pulse

Unlike older single-wavelength lasers, this system combines three distinct wavelengths (755nm, 810nm and 1064nm) into a single pulse. Each wavelength targets a different depth of the hair follicle simultaneously, letting us safely and effectively treat practically all skin types — from light to deep tones — and fine or coarse hair with maximum comfort.

Best for: Long-lasting hair reduction across all skin tones on both face and body.

What to Expect

Duration & Sessions

Session Duration

How long each session takes

Treatment time depends on the area: a small area like the upper lip takes under 5–10 minutes, while larger areas like full legs or back can take 45–60 minutes.

Number of Sessions

How many you'll need

Because hair grows in cycles (anagen, catagen, telogen), lasers only destroy follicles currently in their active growth phase. For optimal long-term clearance, a series of 6 to 8 sessions is typically required, spaced roughly 4 to 6 weeks apart.

Safety

Safe, Comfortable & Effective

Under medical supervision, laser hair reduction is a highly safe daycare procedure — virtually painless, often described as a mild snapping sensation or a cooling prickle. Minor, transient side effects usually resolve within a few hours to a couple of days.

Minor, transient side effects

  • Mild redness (erythema) — a slight flush, similar to a mild sunburn.
  • Perifollicular oedema — slight swelling around the follicles, a positive sign the follicle absorbed the laser energy.
  • Temporary sensitivity — skin may feel slightly tender to the touch immediately after.

Emergency note: While minor redness and a mild sunburnt sensation are expected, contact the clinic immediately if you experience severe pain, unexpected blistering, spreading rashes or signs of an active infection.
