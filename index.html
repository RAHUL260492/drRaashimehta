About the Doctor

Dr. Raashi Mehta

A board-certified Consultant Dermatologist, Dermatosurgeon and Aesthetic Physician with over a decade of clinical excellence across medical dermatology, advanced trichology and high-end aesthetic medicine. She holds an MBBS from Topiwala National Medical College, a DNB in Dermatology from Dr. Balabhai Nanavati Hospital, and an international Fellowship in Dermatologic Laser Surgery from Ramathibodi Hospital, Mahidol University, Bangkok.

Known for meticulous medical precision and a philosophy grounded in natural, graceful outcomes, Dr. Mehta designs bespoke combination treatment plans tailored to each patient's unique physiological blueprint — and performs most procedures herself or under her direct supervision.
