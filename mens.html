For Men

Men's Advanced Grooming & Aesthetics

Men's skin is thicker, denser in collagen and produces more sebum — and daily shaving can compromise the barrier. Our treatments are tailored to male facial anatomy for crisp definition, oil control and high-performance rejuvenation with minimal downtime.

Men's skin has distinct biological characteristics and unique structural aging patterns. Our specialised treatments focus on definition, oil control and rejuvenation with absolute minimal downtime.

01

Men's Executive Medi-Facial & Deep Cleanse

A high-performance treatment for the heavy environmental pollution, deep oil congestion and rough texture common to male skin. It combines advanced Hydrafacial vortex-suction to vacuum out stubborn blackheads and debris, an enzymatic exfoliation to smooth rough skin, and an infusion of high-potency antioxidants and hyaluronic acid to repair skin stressed by daily shaving.

Best for: Deep pore clearing, reducing excess shine, soothing razor burn and restoring a sharp, clean radiance.
02

Laser Beard-Line Contouring & Grooming

For a perpetually neat beard without daily shaving, razor bumps or painful pseudofolliculitis (ingrown hairs). Advanced Triple Wavelength Laser technology precisely targets follicles along the upper cheeks, lower neck and throat line, cleanly shaping the beard contour, permanently reducing stray hairs and thinning coarse growth.

Best for: Eliminating razor bumps, preventing ingrown hairs on the neck and maintaining a crisp, permanent beard line.
03

The Alpha Contour — Jawline & Muscle Toning

A non-invasive structural treatment to reclaim sharp, masculine definition as tissue laxity softens the jawline. It combines Radiofrequency (RF) Tightening to contract loose skin sub-dermally with premium Myolift™ Microcurrent that re-educates and lifts the underlying facial muscles, lifting sagging tissue beneath the chin and sharpening the jawline.

Best for: Sculpting a strong jawline, lifting sagging jowls, reducing a mild double chin and restoring facial firmness.
04

Brotox & Masculine Structural Fillers

Advanced clinical injectables mapped strictly to male facial aesthetics for a well-rested, powerful look without sacrificing natural expression.

  • Brotox (Wrinkle Relaxers) — meticulous micro-injections to soften deep frown lines, heavy forehead furrows and crow's feet, carefully calibrated to preserve the natural lower position of the masculine brow.
  • Structural Dermal Fillers — premium, dense Hyaluronic Acid to enhance a strong chin profile, square a recessed jawline and fill hollow under-eye structures that create a chronically fatigued appearance.
Best for: A rested, defined, naturally masculine look.
05

Exosomes & Scalp PRP

A proactive, multi-pronged clinical approach to male pattern baldness (Androgenetic Alopecia). We pair medical trichology management with Autologous PRP scalp micro-injections and novel exosome therapy: PRP's concentrated growth factors awaken dormant hair roots, prolong the active growth cycle and reverse early crown and hairline thinning, while exosomes act as signalling molecules that stimulate dormant hair cells to produce peptides and growth factors even in resistant cases.

Best for: Receding hairlines, crown thinning and maintaining dense, healthy hair volume.
