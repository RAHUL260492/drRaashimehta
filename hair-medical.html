Hair, Nails & Medical Dermatology

From the root cause of hair loss to chronic skin conditions and infections — compassionate, evidence-based medical care for hair, scalp, nails and skin.

Hair & Scalp

Hair, Scalp & Trichology Solutions

01

Advanced Scalp & Trichology Consultations

A deep dive into your medical history, lifestyle and diet, alongside specialised macroscopic and microscopic examination of your hair shafts and scalp tissue to identify the root cause.

Best for: Identifying the cause of Androgenetic Alopecia (pattern baldness), Telogen Effluvium (excessive shedding), Alopecia Areata, severe dandruff and chronic scalp inflammation.
02

Autologous PRP for Hair Restoration

A clinically proven, natural regenerative therapy using your body's own growth factors to reverse thinning and promote new growth. A blood sample is processed in a centrifuge to isolate concentrated, platelet-rich plasma, then meticulously micro-injected into thinning areas — increasing blood supply to the follicles and extending the active growth phase.

03

Laser Hair Therapies (Low-Level Laser Therapy — LLLT)

A non-invasive, painless light-based therapy using therapeutic, cool-spectrum medical lasers to deliver light energy to the scalp — re-energising cellular activity, decreasing inflammation and halting active shedding.

Nail Care

Specialised Nail Treatments

Nail Fungus (Onychomycosis) Laser Treatment

Advanced medical laser technology safely penetrates the rigid nail plate to reach the underlying nail bed, delivering precise thermal energy that targets and destroys fungal pathogens without damaging surrounding skin or the nail matrix — a safe alternative to long-term oral antifungals.

Core Skin Health

Medical Dermatology

Persistent skin conditions require compassionate, consistent and highly personalised care.

Chronic Inflammatory

Acne, Eczema & Psoriasis

Acne Vulgaris is a medical condition of sebaceous-gland inflammation, treated with targeted topicals, hormonal optimisation and oral retinoids to clear breakouts and prevent scarring. Eczema (dermatitis) involves an impaired barrier and intense itching; we identify triggers, restore the lipid barrier with medical emollients and calm flare-ups. Psoriasis and Lichen Planus are chronic, immune-mediated conditions managed with custom topical regimens, systemic immunomodulators and targeted biologics for severe cases.

Pigment & Allergy

Pigmentation, Allergies & Hives

Pigmentary disorders (melasma, PIH, vitiligo) need careful medical diagnosis of pigment depth before any aesthetic intervention. Skin allergies and Urticaria (hives) cause sudden rashes or itchy welts; we run systematic evaluations to uncover triggers and provide rapid relief with modern non-sedating antihistamines and barrier repair.

Hair & Nail Disorders

Pathological Hair & Nail Conditions

Alopecia Areata (patchy loss), Telogen Effluvium (stress shedding) and scarring alopecias are complex medical disorders treated with targeted scalp therapies, nutritional optimisation and intralesional therapies. Brittle nails, paronychia (nail-fold inflammation) and structural changes are diagnosed to their root cause to save and restore the nail matrix.

Infectious Dermatology

Fungal, Bacterial & Viral Infections

Skin infections spread rapidly if mismanaged with over-the-counter steroid creams; we provide definitive diagnosis and targeted anti-microbial treatment. Fungal: ringworm (tinea), athlete's foot and nail fungus. Bacterial: boils (furuncles), cellulitis and impetigo. Viral: warts, molluscum contagiosum and herpes zoster (shingles).

Specialist Care

Autoimmune Skin Conditions

Conditions where the immune system targets its own skin structures require specialised care, including Lupus Erythematosus (CLE/SLE), Dermatomyositis and blistering diseases such as Pemphigus Vulgaris. We coordinate closely with your broader medical team to implement targeted systemic therapies that quieten the immune response and protect your skin's integrity.
