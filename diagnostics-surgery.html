Core Diagnostics & Dermo-Surgery

Diagnostics & Dermo-Surgical Procedures

Accurate diagnosis is the foundation of effective care. From precise clinical testing to minor daycare surgery under local anaesthesia — comfort, minimal downtime and clean cosmetic outcomes.

Accurate Diagnosis

Advanced Diagnostics & Core Dermatology

We use precise clinical diagnostic procedures to identify underlying skin, hair and nail conditions before curating a treatment plan.

Imaging

Wood's Lamp Examination

A diagnostic tool using transilluminating ultraviolet (black) light. By observing the fluorescence emitted by the skin, we accurately diagnose fungal or bacterial infections, evaluate pigmentary disorders like vitiligo or melasma, and determine the exact depth of pigmentation.

Bedside Test

Scraping for Smears

A quick, painless bedside test where a superficial sample of skin flakes, nail debris or fluid is gently collected and examined under a microscope for immediate identification of fungal elements, parasites or bacterial infections.

Histopathology

Skin, Hair & Nail Biopsies

For complex or atypical conditions, a small tissue sample is safely removed under local anaesthesia and sent for formal histopathological analysis — providing an absolute, microscopic diagnosis for chronic inflammatory disorders, scarring alopecias or unusual growths.

Daycare Dermo-Surgery

Therapeutic & Dermo-Surgical Procedures

For structural lesions, benign growths and reconstructive needs, we perform precise minor surgical and ablative interventions — entirely in a sterile daycare setting under local anaesthesia.

01

Excision of Small Cysts

Safe, complete surgical removal of benign lesions such as sebaceous or epidermoid cysts. The entire cyst wall is removed to eliminate recurrence, using fine cosmetic suturing to minimise scarring.

02

Seborrheic Keratosis & Small BCC Removal

Age-related benign superficial growths (seborrheic keratosis) are cleanly removed with radiofrequency (RF) ablation or electrocautery, leaving skin to heal smoothly. For localised, superficial Basal Cell Carcinomas (BCC), a meticulous excision with clear oncological margins ensures complete cure while preserving the aesthetic contour.

03

Wart & Mucocele Removals

Viral warts (verrucae) are eliminated with precise electrodessication or RF ablation to destroy infected tissue and prevent spread. Mucoceles (benign fluid-filled mucous cysts on the inner lip) are gently excised or ablated to relieve discomfort and restore smooth mucosal texture.

04

Ingrown Nail Surgery & Ear-Lobe Repair

Chronic, painful ingrown toenails are treated with a minor partial matricectomy: the painful nail border is removed and the corresponding root chemically neutralised so the ingrown edge never returns. Ear-lobe repair (lobuloplasty) surgically revises torn, stretched or elongated piercings with ultra-fine sutures, restoring a natural appearance and allowing safe re-piercing later.

05

Advanced Vitiligo Surgeries

For stable vitiligo that has resisted creams or light therapy, surgical intervention offers remarkable repigmentation. Using advanced grafting techniques (punch grafting, ultra-thin epidermal grafting or cellular suspensions), healthy melanocytes are harvested from a normal donor site and transplanted onto stable white patches to restore natural colour.

Precise diagnosis, careful hands

Most diagnostic tests and minor procedures are completed comfortably in a single daycare visit under local anaesthesia — with minimal downtime and clean, cosmetic healing.
