Meet the Doctor

Dr. Raashi Mehta

Consultant Dermatologist, Dermatosurgeon & Aesthetic Physician — blending evidence-based medical science with a personalised aesthetic vision.

Over a Decade of Clinical Excellence

A dermatologist's dermatologist

Dr. Raashi Mehta is a highly accomplished, board-certified Consultant Dermatologist and Dermatosurgeon with over a decade of clinical excellence in core medical dermatology, advanced trichology and high-end aesthetic medicine. Armed with an MBBS from Topiwala National Medical College and a DNB in Dermatology from the prestigious Dr. Balabhai Nanavati Hospital, Mumbai, her passion for skin health led her to pursue an international Fellowship in Dermatologic Laser Surgery at Ramathibodi Hospital, Mahidol University in Bangkok, Thailand.

As the founder of Dr. Raashi Mehta's Skin Clinic and a Senior Consultant at major healthcare institutions like Bombay Hospital, she treats the full spectrum of hair, nail and skin health. Her experience spans specialised paediatric dermatology, high-stakes autoimmune and infectious cases, and advanced regenerative and anti-ageing treatments.

Known for her meticulous medical precision and a philosophy grounded in natural, graceful outcomes, Dr. Mehta creates bespoke, combination treatment plans tailored to each patient's unique physiological blueprint.

Our Clinic Philosophy

Healthy skin is the foundation of beautiful skin

Our mission is to blend advanced, evidence-based medical science with a personalised aesthetic vision — delivering results that look completely natural and respect your unique anatomy. It rests on three foundational pillars.

01

Evidence-Based Medical Precision

We view the skin not just cosmetically, but as a complex biological ecosystem. Every treatment — from a clinical diagnostic procedure to an advanced aesthetic intervention — is grounded in rigorous science, performed under strict medical supervision, and prioritises your skin's health, structural integrity and long-term safety above all else.

02

Tailored Combination Therapy

No two skin profiles are identical, and lasting change cannot come from a one-size-fits-all checklist. We design multi-modality plans — intelligently layering advanced lasers with cellular regenerative therapies and targeted dermo-surgical techniques — to address concerns from multiple depths for truly transformative results.

03

Graceful, Natural Outcomes

The best aesthetic work is completely undetectable. We reject artificial, over-treated or exaggerated trends in favour of structural rejuvenation and subtle enhancement that honours your natural expressions — restoring a refreshed, vibrant and confident version of yourself at every stage of life.

"Healthy skin is the foundation of beautiful skin. Our mission is to blend advanced, evidence-based medical science with a personalised aesthetic vision, delivering results that look completely natural and respect your unique anatomy."

Dr. Raashi Mehta
